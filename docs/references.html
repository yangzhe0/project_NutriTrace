https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-025-01159-0

[6] NHS. Tiredness and fatigue: causes, assessment and seeking care.

持续疲劳的原因、就医时机及评估方向，用于身体线索整理与就医准备；原型规则不等同于经验证的临床分诊系统。

[7] NHS. Iron deficiency anaemia: symptoms and clinical assessment.

https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/iron-deficiency-anaemia/
